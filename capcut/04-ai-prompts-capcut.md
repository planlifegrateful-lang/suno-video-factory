# CapCut AI Prompts – Optimized for Reels

Use these inside CapCut’s AI tools (AI Video, AI Image, Text-to-Video where available).

---

## Base Cinematic Prompt (best results)

```
Cinematic vertical video, 9:16, soft golden hour light, deep emerald and gold tones, peaceful but powerful atmosphere, young person or silhouette looking toward the sky with hope, gentle wind, subtle light particles, emotional, spiritual strength, photorealistic, film grain, shallow depth of field, smooth slow camera push-in, high quality
```

---

## Variations by Emotion

**Gratitude / Alhamdulillah**  
Add: `warm sunrise light, hand over heart, peaceful smile, soft clouds, feeling of thankfulness`

**Resilience / Still Standing**  
Add: `person walking forward as storm clears into sunlight, strong posture, determined, overcoming`

**New Beginning / Bismillah**  
Add: `dawn light, first step on a path, hopeful, fresh start energy, soft mist`

**Du’a / Ya Rabb**  
Add: `quiet moment of prayer, hands slightly raised, soft ambient light, deep emotional connection, serene`

**Limitless**  
Add: `standing on high ground looking at vast open landscape, free mind, unlimited possibility, expansive sky`

---

## CapCut-Specific Tips

- Keep prompts under 200–250 characters when possible (some AI tools truncate)
- Always specify `vertical 9:16` and `cinematic`
- Avoid asking for text inside the AI video (add text later in CapCut)
- Generate 2–3 versions and pick the most emotional one
