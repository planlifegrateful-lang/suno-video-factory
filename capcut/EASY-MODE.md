# CapCut EASY MODE
### Make a Reel in 7 minutes. No thinking required.

Just follow the numbers. Do not skip. Do not invent.

---

## BEFORE YOU START (do this once)

1. Open CapCut
2. Create a new project → choose **9:16**
3. Save this project as **"MASTER TEMPLATE"** so you can duplicate it forever

---

## EVERY TIME YOU MAKE A REEL

### Step 1 – Duplicate (10 seconds)
- Open your **MASTER TEMPLATE**
- Duplicate it
- Rename it to the track name (example: Alhamdulillah)

### Step 2 – Drop Audio (30 seconds)
- Tap the audio icon
- Import your Suno track
- Trim it to the best 15–22 seconds (the hook + emotional part)

### Step 3 – Drop Video (1 minute)
- Import your background video (or generate one with the prompt below)
- Stretch or trim so it matches the audio length

**Quick AI Video Prompt (copy-paste):**
```
Cinematic vertical 9:16, soft golden light, emerald and gold tones, peaceful powerful mood, person looking at sky with hope, gentle wind, light particles, emotional, photorealistic, smooth slow camera
```

### Step 4 – Auto Captions (1 minute)
- Tap **Text → Captions → Auto Captions**
- Language: English
- Style: White text + black outline, bold font, medium-large size
- Position: lower third

### Step 5 – Add 2 Text Lines (2 minutes)

**Line 1 – Hook (appears at 0.5 seconds)**  
Copy one of these:
- Alhamdulillah for this test
- Ease is already written
- Still standing
- I planned. Now I trust.
- Bismillah. Begin again.

Style: Big, white, black outline, upper or center screen

**Line 2 – Emotional Peak (appears around middle of video)**  
Copy one of these:
- Alhamdulillah for this difficulty — ease is already written for me
- Hasbunallahu wa ni’mal wakeel
- I refuse despair. Your mercy is greater
- Ya Allah, increase me in every good

### Step 6 – End Card (1 minute)
At the last 3–4 seconds add:

```
[Track Name]
@planlifegrateful
```

Centered. Clean. Hold until the end.

### Step 7 – Export (30 seconds)
- Resolution: 1080x1920
- Frame rate: 30
- Quality: High
- Export

Done.
