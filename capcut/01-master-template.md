# CapCut Master Template – 15–25 Second Reel

Copy this structure every single time. Do not invent new layouts.

---

## Project Settings

- Ratio: **9:16**
- Duration: 15–25 seconds (never longer than 30s for first posts)
- Frame rate: 30 fps

---

## Layer Order (Bottom → Top)

1. **Background Video** (cinematic AI video or your footage)
2. **Audio** (Suno track – the star)
3. **Auto Captions** (from CapCut)
4. **Emotional Text Lines** (manual, big, timed)
5. **End Card** (track name + @planlifegrateful)

---

## Exact Timeline Structure

| Time | What Happens |
|------|--------------|
| 0.0 – 0.5s | Black or soft fade-in (gives algorithm breathing room) |
| 0.5 – 3.0s | Strong visual + **Hook Text** appears |
| 3.0 – 12s | Main visual + auto-captions of lyrics or spoken line |
| 12 – 18s | Peak emotional line (grateful language) |
| 18 – 22s | End card fades in |
| 22 – 25s | Hold end card + soft audio fade |

---

## How to Save as Reusable Template

### CapCut Desktop
1. Build the project once with placeholders
2. File → Save as Template (or export the project file)
3. Next time: Open template → replace audio + background video only

### CapCut Mobile
1. Finish one perfect Reel
2. Tap “…” → “Save as Template” (if available in your region)
3. Or just duplicate the project every time and swap media

---

## Placeholder Strategy

Keep these two placeholders in your master template:

- `AUDIO_PLACEHOLDER` (silent or tone) → replace with Suno track
- `VIDEO_PLACEHOLDER` (solid color or simple loop) → replace with AI video or footage

This is how you go from 20 minutes to 6–8 minutes per Reel.
