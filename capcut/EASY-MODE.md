## BEFORE YOU START (do this once)

1. Open CapCut (mobile or desktop)
2. New project → **9:16** → 30 fps
3. Build the layer stack from [01-master-template.md](01-master-template.md)
4. Save / name the project **MASTER TEMPLATE**
5. Keep two placeholders: `AUDIO_PLACEHOLDER` and `VIDEO_PLACEHOLDER`

If your region has **Save as Template**, use it. Otherwise duplicate the project every time.

---

## EVERY TIME YOU MAKE A REEL

### Step 1 – Duplicate (10 seconds)
- Open **MASTER TEMPLATE**
- Duplicate
- Rename to the track name (example: `GratefulMorning`)

### Step 2 – Drop Audio (30 seconds)
- Replace `AUDIO_PLACEHOLDER` with the rights-checked Suno track
- Trim to the best **15–22 seconds** (hook + peak)
- Leave **0.5s** of quiet at the start
- If you speak on camera: duck the track under your voice

### Step 3 – Drop Video (1 minute)
- Replace `VIDEO_PLACEHOLDER` so video length = audio length
- Grateful Morning visual prompt: [../prompts/packs/grateful-morning-visual.md](../prompts/packs/grateful-morning-visual.md)
- Generic prompt if you have no pack:

```
Cinematic vertical 9:16, soft golden light, emerald and gold tones, peaceful powerful mood, hopeful atmosphere, gentle wind, light particles, emotional, photorealistic, smooth camera
```

### Step 4 – Auto Captions (1 minute)
- Text → Captions → Auto Captions → English
- Style: white + black outline, bold, lower third
- Full settings: [05-auto-caption-settings.md](05-auto-caption-settings.md)
- Fix wrong words. Delete captions on pure instrumental.

### Step 5 – Two text lines (2 minutes)
Copy from [QUICK-TEXT.md](QUICK-TEXT.md). Do not rewrite.
- **Line 1 hook at 0.5s** (upper or center)
- **Line 2 peak ~8–15s**
- Style: big, white, black outline

### Step 6 – End card (1 minute)
Last 3–4 seconds, centered:
```
Track title
@planlifegrateful
```

### Step 7 – Export (30 seconds)
- 1080×1920 · 30 fps · High · MP4 H.264
- Full checklist: [06-export-settings.md](06-export-settings.md)
- Watch it once on a phone before requesting approval

**No public posting until rights and approval gates pass.**
