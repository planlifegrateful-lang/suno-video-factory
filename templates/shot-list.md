# Shot List – 15-Second Viral Template

Use this exact structure for every video.

---

## Timeline

| Time | Visual | Text on Screen | Audio |
|------|--------|----------------|-------|
| 0-2s | Strong visual / face / scene | Hook line (big) | Track intro or silence |
| 2-8s | Main scene / you speaking | Supporting line or lyrics | Track builds |
| 8-12s | Emotional peak | Grateful language line | Hook of the song |
| 12-15s | End card / logo / CTA | Track name + @handle | Song continues or fade |

---

## On-Screen Text Rules

- Max 6-8 words per line
- High contrast (white + black outline or gold)
- Appear word-by-word or line-by-line for retention
- Never cover the face
