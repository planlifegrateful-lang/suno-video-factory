# UGC Scripts – Ready to Film

Record these on your phone. 15-30 seconds. Natural energy. No over-acting.

## Script 1 – "The Hard Day" (Highest converting)

**Hook (0-3s):**  
"I used to say ‘why is this happening to me’ every single day…"

**Body:**  
"Then I started saying this instead:  
‘Alhamdulillah for this difficulty — ease is already written for me.’  

Same problems. Completely different heart."

## Filming Tips

- Film in natural light (window or golden hour)
- Hold phone vertical, eye level
- Speak like you’re talking to one close friend
- Leave 0.5s silence at the start for the algorithm
- Add the actual Suno track as background audio (ducked under your voice)
