# CapCut Auto Caption Settings – Maximum Retention

These settings make people keep watching.

---

## Recommended Caption Style

- **Font**: Montserrat Bold / CapCut Bold Sans / Impact
- **Size**: Medium-large (readable on mobile without zooming)
- **Color**: White
- **Stroke / Outline**: Black, thickness 4–8
- **Shadow**: Soft black shadow (optional, helps on bright videos)
- **Position**: Lower third (but not covering faces or important action)
- **Animation**: None or very subtle fade (too much animation kills retention)

---

## Timing Rules

- Captions should appear slightly before or exactly with the spoken/sung word
- Never let captions lag more than 0.2–0.3 seconds
- Break long lines into 4–7 words max
- Highlight the most emotional words in gold or light green if CapCut allows

---

## Workflow

1. Add Suno audio
2. Tap “Captions” → “Auto Captions”
3. Choose language (English)
4. Apply the style above to all
5. Manually fix any wrong words (Suno lyrics can confuse the AI)
6. Delete captions that appear during pure instrumental parts if they look empty

---

## Pro Move

After auto captions are styled once, copy the entire caption track and paste into the next project. Then only fix the words. This saves massive time in batch mode.
