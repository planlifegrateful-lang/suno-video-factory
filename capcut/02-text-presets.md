# CapCut Text Presets – Ready to Copy

Use these exact lines. Do not rewrite on the fly.

---

## Hook Lines (0.5 – 3 seconds)

Pick one per video:

- Alhamdulillah for this test
- Ease is already written
- I planned. Now I trust.
- Hasbunallah.
- Still standing.
- Bismillah. Begin again.
- Ya Allah, increase me.
- This difficulty is temporary.

**Style in CapCut:**
- Font: Montserrat Bold or CapCut “Impact” / “Bold Sans”
- Size: Large (covers ~30–40% width)
- Color: White
- Stroke: Black, 4–6px
- Animation: Fade in or Typewriter (fast)
- Position: Upper third or center

---

## Peak Emotional Lines (middle of video)

- Alhamdulillah for this difficulty — ease is already written for me.
- I am grateful You are enough for me.
- I refuse despair. Your mercy is greater.
- This result is from You alone.
- Ya Allah, I am grateful. Increase me in every good.

**Style:**
- Slightly smaller than hook
- Same white + black stroke
- Appear word-by-word or line-by-line for retention

Line 1: Track Title

**Style:**

---

## Pro CapCut Trick

Create a “Text Preset” project:
1. Make a short project with all these text styles already set
2. Copy-paste the text layers into every new Reel
3. Only change the actual words

This removes 70% of the design work.
